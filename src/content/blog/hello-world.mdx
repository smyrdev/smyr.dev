It’s a Sunday in the middle of August 2026. After a lot of attempts, small writings, and every kind of procrastination skill that exists, today I sat down and booked a block in my calendar to finally start writing my thoughts on my own blog. I’ve tried to write my journal in a small book since 2024, but that was for personal use—writing about my days, moods, experiences, and plans. But I never had the confidence to write in public, though I made a few attempts when I was a teenager, and once or twice several years ago. I wrote once, left it forever, and never came back. But I’m back again. What’s different this time? Let’s see if I stick to it. Later, I’ll write about some interesting stuff: my current habits, and life. I may update this post or write a new one.
