## Problem

The monolith was not the problem. The problem was that every change to it was a
negotiation with every other team, and the only way to deploy was to stop.

## Approach

Read paths moved first because they are reversible. Each new service ran in
shadow mode against live traffic, and its answers were diffed against the
monolith's until the disagreement rate was flat at zero.

Only then did writes move, one aggregate at a time, behind a flag that could be
flipped back inside a request.
